Part B
What is included in the application:
Resource	What it represents
Users	People who have accounts and access LiteStock
Businesses	The shop/business using LiteStock
Products	Items the business sells
Categories	Groups of products, e.g. Beverages, Snacks
Suppliers	Companies/people supplying products
Purchases	Stock purchased from suppliers
Purchase Items	Individual products within a purchase
Sales	Completed customer transactions
Sale Items	Individual products within a sale
Customers	Customers, if the business chooses to record them
Inventory / Stock	Current quantity and stock status of products
Stock Adjustments	Manual additions/removals/corrections to stock
Payments	Payment information associated with sales/purchases
Expenses	Business expenses recorded in the system
Notifications	Alerts such as low-stock warnings
Reports	Generated sales, inventory, profit, and business reports
Activity Logs	Records of important actions performed by users
Roles / Permissions	Access-control information determining what users can do
